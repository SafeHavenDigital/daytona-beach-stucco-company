## Phase 4–5: Pillars and Validation

### Pillar 1 — Stucco Problems & Diagnosis (Symptom Pillar) ★ CRITICAL
> The house pillar for FIND THE WATER FIRST.

- Search volume: **PASS** — `~high`. Deep PAA, dozens of competing national articles, rich autocomplete. Real demand.
- Market-centric: **PASS** — this is the market's own language ("crack in stucco wall"), not the contractor's ("remediation").
- Competitive: **PASS** — top results are thin, out-of-state, undifferentiated. Zero local competition. Winnable with FL coastal specificity.
- Proprietary advantage: **YES** — diagnosis-first is the stated position, and open-wall photos are a proof asset no competitor has published.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR — highest priority.**

### Pillar 2 — Stucco Cost & Pricing ★ CRITICAL
- Search volume: **PASS** — `~very high`, strongest commercial-informational intent in the category.
- Market-centric: **PASS** — cost is the #1 unanswered question in every PAA set.
- Competitive: **PASS with a caveat** — aggregators own the national term and a Central FL competitor already owns "Florida." **Do not target the state term.** Target Volusia/Daytona county-level, where nothing exists.
- Proprietary advantage: **YES, conditional** — requires the contractor to actually publish ranges. Without real numbers this pillar cannot ship.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR — gated on `[PRICE RANGES]`.**

### Pillar 3 — Services (Money Pages) ★ CRITICAL
- Search volume: **PASS** — highest transactional intent; the pages that convert.
- Market-centric: **PASS**.
- Competitive: **PARTIAL FAIL organically** — a competitor owns a tight silo on the head term. Passes anyway because these pages are non-optional conversion infrastructure and are the landing target for local pack, GBP, and paid traffic.
- Proprietary advantage: **YES** — diagnosis-first framing differentiates identical service pages.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR — build for conversion, not for near-term organic.**

### Pillar 4 — Service Areas (Local/Geo) — DEMOTED TO CONDITIONAL CLUSTER (2026-08-30)
- Search volume: **PASS** — `~medium` per city, meaningful in aggregate across 10 municipalities.
- Market-centric: **PASS** — "near me" and city-modified searches are the local default.
- Competitive: **PASS** — directories rank, meaning weak content is winning. Genuinely winnable fast.
- Proprietary advantage: **FAIL (re-scored 2026-08-30)** — was CONDITIONAL on each page carrying
  real, distinct local substance. The condition was never met. `[NEIGHBORHOODS WORKED]` and
  `[PHOTOS WITH LOCATION NOTED]` remain outstanding, and the flagship page shipped with its
  differentiating section written from general Daytona exposure facts, naming no worked
  neighborhood.
- **PROTOCOL VERDICT: fails 1 of 4 tests — below the 2-test threshold SKILL.md requires for
  automatic demotion.**
- **RECORDED VERDICT: DEMOTED TO CONDITIONAL CLUSTER on owner direction (2026-08-30).** The
  protocol result and the override are recorded separately and deliberately. The pillar's own
  standing gate — "a city page without real local substance should not be published; ten thin
  duplicated pages will hurt a new domain" — pointed the same way as advice; the owner rule makes
  it binding.

**Owner architecture rule governing this pillar (2026-08-30, verbatim):**
> A geographic modifier by itself does not justify a separate URL. Do not create multiple versions
> of the same service page simply by changing the city, such as separate Stucco Repair + city
> pages, when the underlying service/search intent is substantially the same.

**Not a permanent prohibition.** A future location page may be considered individually only if the
`/keyword-research` protocol validates sufficiently distinct search intent AND there is enough
genuine, unique local substance to justify a useful standalone page rather than thin or duplicated
content. No city page is pre-approved. Each requires its own splitting test.

### Pillar 5 — Commercial, Condo & Compliance ★ HIGH (time-sensitive)
- Search volume: **CONDITIONAL PASS** — `~low volume, extreme value`. Small denominator; a single condo contract dwarfs dozens of homeowner jobs.
- Market-centric: **PASS** — boards and managers genuinely search these statute terms.
- Competitive: **PASS — strongest in the plan.** Zero contractors on the SERP.
- Proprietary advantage: **CONDITIONAL** — requires commercial licensing, insurance limits, and occupied-building capability the contractor must confirm.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR — deadline-driven, build early despite low volume.**

### Pillar 6 — Inspection, Insurance & Selling — HIGH
- Search volume: **PASS** — `~high` on 4-point inspection terms.
- Market-centric: **PASS**.
- Competitive: **PASS** — insurance and inspection sites rank; no contractor addresses the repair side.
- Proprietary advantage: **YES** — the deadline-and-relief framing is directly on-position.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR.**

### Pillar 7 — Finish, Texture & Appearance — MEDIUM
- Search volume: **PARTIAL** — `~low-medium` locally; mostly national DIY intent.
- Market-centric: **PASS**.
- Competitive: **PASS** — the DIY articles that rank actively help us (they end at "this is hard, hire someone").
- Proprietary advantage: **YES** — texture matching is an ownable, falsifiable craft claim.
- **VERDICT: VALID PILLAR — demoted to medium. Visual proof more than search traffic.**

### DEMOTED / REMOVED
- **"Coastal / salt air stucco"** — proposed as a pillar; **DEMOTED TO CLUSTER.** Fails market-centric (people search symptoms, not "salt air stucco") and fails advantage (positioning.md flags the claim as already saturated). Becomes a *section* inside city and symptom pages, never a pillar.
- **"Why choose us / our process"** — **REMOVED as an SEO pillar.** Product-centric. Ships as an About/Process page for conversion, targeting nothing.

**Architecture rules:**
1. **Money pages sit at root depth.** `/stucco-repair/` is never nested — shortest path, strongest signal.
1b. **One authoritative page per genuinely distinct service/search intent.** `/stucco-repair/` is the
    single authoritative page for stucco repair **services, costs, Daytona Beach geo intent,
    contractor/company intent, and crack-repair intent.** A keyword variation alone — geographic
    wording, contractor/company wording, cost/price/estimate wording, or a narrower noun for the
    same work — never earns its own URL. Consolidations to date: cost 2026-08-28;
    Daytona Beach geo and crack repair 2026-08-30. **Do not re-split any of them.**
1c. **The homepage is the company entity page.** `/` represents Daytona Beach Stucco Company and the
    broader verified service offering — positioning, trust, and the range of services, linking out
    to each authoritative service page. It is **not** a Stucco Repair landing page and must not
    compete with `/stucco-repair/` for repair intent. It owns contractor/company entity queries.
2. **Supporting content is foldered** (`/problems/`, `/guides/`) so the URL itself declares intent and keeps the money namespace clean.
3. **No blog.** There is no `/blog/`. Every supporting piece is an evergreen guide with a permanent URL. Dated blog structure is dead weight for a local service site.
4. **The commercial funnel is physically separated** under `/commercial-stucco/`, per positioning.md: never blend deadline messaging into homeowner pages.

## Market-Level Facts (attributed — usable as cited context, never as this contractor's claim)

- National stucco repair ranges reported by third parties: ~$8–$50/sq ft; Florida traditional ~$10–$35/sq ft, EIFS ~$15–$55/sq ft; Central Florida typical standard repair ~$1,000–$2,500; moisture-involved repairs ~$4,000–$10,000+; full re-stucco ~$15,000–$30,000. *(Sources: Angi, HomeGuide, Homewyse, cfstuccoandpainting.com, 2026.)* **Cite as "industry sources report," never as our pricing.**
- Florida milestone inspection: Phase 2 follows a Phase 1 finding of substantial structural deterioration within 180 days; **required repairs must begin within 365 days of the Phase 2 report** unless the local building department requires earlier. *(FS 553.899.)*
- **SIRS funding plans due December 31, 2026**, covering roof, load-bearing structure, waterproofing, exterior painting, and foundation. *(FS 718.112(2)(g).)*
- Crack severity conventions widely published: hairline <1/16" generally cosmetic; >1/16" warrants professional evaluation; diagonal/stair-step patterns, efflorescence, or growth over time indicate a deeper cause.
- EIFS vs hardcoat identification: EIFS sounds hollow/drummy and dents under pressure; hardcoat is solid through its thickness.

## Method actually applied

Refresh Mode → Option ② (add a new topic area). The existing plan was read first and treated as
the baseline of record. Owner keywords were mapped onto existing clusters BEFORE any new page was
considered. A new URL was proposed only where the **service delivered** differs, not merely where
the wording differs.

### The splitting test used on every group

A group earns a standalone URL only if it passes **3 of 4**:

1. **Different work.** A different crew-day, different materials, or a different deliverable —
   not the same repair described with a different noun.
2. **Different buyer state.** The searcher wants a different outcome, not the same outcome.
3. **SERP separation.** The top results for the group are visibly different pages from the top
   results for `stucco repair`, i.e. Google itself treats it as its own thing.
4. **Standalone substance.** At least ~800 words of non-duplicated, verifiable content exist
   without inventing business facts.

Groups failing this test were merged as an **H2 + FAQ block on an existing page**, which is
stronger than a thin page: it deepens a URL that is already being linked and already ranks.
