## Recommended Outline
- **H1:** Cracks in Stucco: Which Ones Matter and Which Ones Don't
- H2: Most stucco cracks are normal. Here's the honest version.
  - Widely published convention: hairline under 1/16" is generally cosmetic — result of settling, thermal movement, cement shrinkage. **Attribute as industry convention, not as our measurement standard.**
- H2: A field guide to crack patterns
  - Hairline / spider — usually cosmetic
  - Straight vertical or horizontal at a joint — movement, usually manageable
  - Diagonal from window and door corners — stress, look closer
  - Stair-step — investigate, may be structural
  - Cracking with staining, efflorescence, or a soft edge — water is involved
- H2: The three things that turn a cosmetic crack into an expensive one
- H2: What "over 1/16 inch" actually means with a business card in your hand
- H2: Why Florida coastal walls crack differently
  - Thermal cycling, wind-driven rain, humidity — but per positioning.md, must go past the saturated observation to the specific detailing consequence.
- H2: Can you just paint over it? (No, and here's what happens when you do)
- H2: When to call someone
- H2: If you already had it patched and it came back → strong link out
