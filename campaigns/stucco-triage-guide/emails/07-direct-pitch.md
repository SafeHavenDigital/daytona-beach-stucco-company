## Email Copy

Last email about the Moisture Check, then I'll leave you alone until your
90-day reminder.

Three reasons people don't book it. All three are reasonable.

**"I'll just get a repair quote — that's free."**

You should, and get several. But a repair quote answers *what would you charge
to fix what you can see.* It doesn't answer whether there's water in the wall,
because the person quoting it usually hasn't measured. If the wall is dry,
you've lost nothing by finding out cheaply. If it's wet, you've just avoided
buying a patch that was going to fail. The quote and the diagnosis are
different purchases.

**"$300 to $500 is a lot to be told about my own wall."**

It is real money, and I'm not going to argue you out of that. What I'd say is
that it's the smallest number in this entire situation. A patch is more. A
patch that fails and turns into opened wall, replaced sheathing, and framing
work is considerably more. The diagnostic is the cheapest thing you'll buy
here, and it's the one that determines whether the expensive things are
necessary.

**"I'll wait and see if it gets worse."**

Here's the one where I think you're often right.

If you're Pattern 1 or Pattern 2 with no staining, no soft spots, no bubbling —
waiting is genuinely the correct call. Take the measurement, note the date,
look again in six months. That's what the Wall Record is for. I'd rather you
did that than paid us out of vague worry.

Where waiting costs you is Pattern 4. Water in a wall doesn't pause. Every
month it's in there is more lath, more paper, more sheathing, and eventually
framing. The wall doesn't announce when it crosses from a repair into a
rebuild — that line gets crossed quietly, and it's only visible in hindsight.

So: **if you have staining, softness, bubbling paint, efflorescence, or a musty
smell inside against that wall, this is worth doing now.** If you don't, it
probably isn't, and I'd rather say that than take the booking.

**[ Book a Stucco Moisture Check ]** — $300 to $500

If you're in the "not yet" group, nothing further is needed from you. Your
90-day reminder is scheduled and it'll turn up on its own.

Thanks for reading all of these.
